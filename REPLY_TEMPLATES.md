# RingRoute USA — Inbound Reply Playbook

**Use these to respond within 2 hours of any reply.** Cold-email intent decays fast — every hour you wait cuts your conversion rate ~10%. Paste, swap the brackets, send.

**Voice rules (read once, then ignore the rules and just sound human):**
- Short. 1-3 sentences for the first reply, never a wall.
- Acknowledge what they actually said. No generic "thanks for reaching out."
- Move toward a calendar link or a phone number, not more email back-and-forth.
- One ask per reply. Never two CTAs in one message.
- No corporate words: "leverage", "synergy", "circle back", "touch base", "loop in", "value-add".
- Sign off "Angel" — not "Best, Angel L., Founder of RingRoute USA, etc."

- Demo recording: `[YOUR 60-SEC DEMO LINK]` ← Loom or hosted MP4 of what the product sounds like answering a fake restaurant call. Record once, use forever.


## 3 — STALL 😐 ("we already have something" / "we use [competitor]" / "we don't really miss calls")

**Trigger phrases:** "we already have", "we use X", "we don't have that problem", "our staff handles it"

```
[First name], fair — most places have *something*. The thing ours catches that voicemail and answering services miss: the 11pm and 2am calls. Those are usually next-day reservations or "are you still open" — and they almost always go to a competitor.

60 seconds to hear what that looks like: [YOUR 60-SEC DEMO LINK]

If your current setup handles those calls, ignore me. If not, here's the calendar: [YOUR CAL.COM LINK]

Angel
```

## Triage rule of thumb

When a reply hits your inbox:

1. **Read the first sentence.** That's the bucket.
2. **Match to a template above** (or closest one).
3. **Edit the brackets, swap 2-3 words to sound personal**, don't rewrite the whole thing.
4. **Send within 2 hours of receiving** — set a phone notification on your inbox.
5. **If they reply again** → keep it conversational, drop the templates. By the second exchange you're a human, not a script.
